Features

See it working.

Not mockups — this is SleetPOS running in a real store, doing the job it does every day.

Checkout

Everything you need accessible from one screen.

Scan, tap, or search up items within seconds. Speeding up your checkout lines means more business.

Cash & card pricing

Two prices. No surprises.

Every sale shows the cash price and the card price side by side — right on the screen your customer is watching. They pick how to pay, and the register charges exactly what the screen says.

Inventory

Counts that keep themselves.

Every sale updates your stock on its own. Check what you have, what is running low, and what sold today — from the register, the dashboard, or your phone.

iPhone app

Your store, in your pocket.

Check today's sales, your top sellers, and the whole week from anywhere. Scan barcodes with your phone's camera to update products and restock — right from the aisle.
