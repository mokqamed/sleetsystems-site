What exactly does the $700 include?

The POS system, cash drawer, barcode scanner, label printer, receipt printer, and payment terminal. With the outright purchase, you own all six pieces.

Are there any monthly fees?

There is no monthly standard POS software fee while you process with SleetSystems. Payment processing is separate. Optional services have their own charges: for example, our connected website has a $75 monthly upkeep fee. Hardware lease payments also apply if you choose to lease an upgrade.

How do you set my processing rate?

We review your past processing statements and quote a rate for your business. We’ll explain the offer before you sign up.

What happens if I stop processing with you?

Hardware you purchased belongs to you. Continuing to use our cloud software requires a separate monthly subscription and a supported payment setup. For placement equipment, return the POS system and payment terminal within two weeks after ending service to avoid equipment fees. Any remaining equipment payment obligations follow your agreement.

Does SleetPOS work for cafés?

Yes, cafés and coffee shops are part of the businesses we serve. We can walk through menu items and options, checkout, and optional equipment such as a kitchen display. Your demo is the place to confirm the exact workflow your café needs.

Can you build a website or a custom feature?

Yes. We offer connected business websites and custom software projects. A website connected to the POS with online payments is $300 to set up and $75 per month for upkeep and menu changes. Other websites and custom features are scoped and quoted for your business.
