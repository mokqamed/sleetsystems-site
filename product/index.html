The modern register for bodegas.

SleetPOS is an all-in-one point of sale (POS) system: fast checkout, live inventory, cash and card pricing, and a second screen that faces your customer.

Built in New York for bodegas, grocery stores, smoke shops, delis, boutiques and more.

iPhone app

Your store, in your pocket.

Check today's sales, your top sellers, and the whole week from anywhere. Scan barcodes with your phone's camera to update products and restock — right from the aisle.

Invoice scanning

Photograph the invoice. The products fill themselves in.

Take a picture of a supplier invoice and SleetPOS reads the lines — item, quantity and cost — then lines them up against the products you already carry.

You look it over and approve it before anything changes — and it remembers your answers, so the next invoice from that supplier fills in on its own.

Shelf labels

Every price on the shelf, printed from the comfort of your home.

Send shelf labels from the dashboard or your phone and the register at the store prints them — item name, barcode, and both the cash and card price. Change a price and reprint in seconds.
