Talk through a connected website
  1. 01 / Discover

    Browse your website.

    A customer finds your business and opens your online menu.

  2. 02 / Order

    Choose, pay, pick a time.

    They select available items and a pickup time, then pay through online checkout.

  3. 03 / Prepare

    Your register gets the order.

    Your team accepts the order at the designated register and prepares it for pickup.

  4. 04 / Collect

    Hand it over. Record the sale.

    Staff mark the order ready, then picked up. The completed sale is recorded in SleetPOS.

This is the planned pickup workflow, not a live feature today. A standalone business website can be scoped now; connected ordering is a separate service with its own launch timing and charges.
