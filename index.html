How we build

The principles behind our software.

A small set of beliefs that guide everything we ship.

Trust by default

Every transaction is recorded on tamper-evident, append-only ledgers — audit-ready records, every day, without effort.

Speed at the counter

Every interaction is built for the busiest minute of the busiest hour. If it doesn't make a cashier faster, it doesn't ship.

Compliant communication

Customer messaging is opt-in, auditable, and revocable. STOP always works. The audit trail is complete.

No lock-in

Your data is yours. Switching off our software should be as fair as switching to it was.

About Sleet Systems

Sleet Systems Inc is a corporation registered in the State of New York, headquartered in Yonkers. We are an independent software vendor — we build and license software.

We work with retailers who own their own equipment and customer relationships, and want software that respects both. Today our flagship product is SleetPOS. Tomorrow there will be more.

If you're a retailer interested in SleetPOS, or you'd like to talk to us about something else entirely, we'd love to hear from you.
