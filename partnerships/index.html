Partnerships

Grow with SleetPOS.

We are looking for people who can sell, people who know store owners ready for a better register, and products that want to connect with ours.

Sell SleetPOS

You bring the store owners. We handle the software, the setup, and the support. SleetPOS runs in real stores today — what you show an owner is what they get at the counter.

Know a store owner?

You do not have to be a salesperson. If you know someone who owns a store and needs a new register, an introduction is enough — we will take it from there.

Integrations

If you make software or hardware for stores and want it to work with SleetPOS, we want to hear about it. Tell us what your product does and how you picture the two connecting.

Independent contract work

Selling and referral partnerships are 1099 contract work, not employment. You set your own pace, and we agree on terms up front before anything starts.
